Scientific sources

Atmospheres and conditions: NASA facts for Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

Distances use JPL Table 1 J2000 semi-major axes, approximating Earth with the Earth/Moon barycenter. We multiply the absolute difference from Earth's axis by 149,597,870.7 km/AU and round to the nearest million kilometres. This is an illustrative orbital-radius gap, not current separation, a time average, a closest approach, or a flight path. Journey time divides that distance by a fictional constant speed of 100,000 km/h, without acceleration or orbital navigation. Earth is a staycation.
