# Planetary Travel Guide

A personal astronomy catalogue that starts with the eight solar-system planets as holiday destinations and lets you add your own planet entries.

## The Unique Kernel
Learn planetary science by imagining a holiday: explore a destination, then click “Book the package” to reveal “Travel booked!”, a journey estimate, things to bring, and a playful survival verdict grounded in the planet's conditions.

## Who It's For
The learner themselves: someone who enjoys reading about cosmology and planets and wants to go deeper through a fun experience that makes the information memorable.

## The Core Loop
Browse or search for a planet, open its destination profile, explore its conditions and what being there would feel like, then reveal the fictional holiday verdict. Return to explore another planet or update information as learning continues.

## Inspiration & Identity
A playful planetary holiday guide with real astronomy underneath. The learner proposed a “Welcome to your planetary travel guide” banner, “Explore destinations,” a search field saying “Enter your holiday destination,” and destination cards. No specific visual reference or aesthetic has been selected.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Make learning astronomy fun rather than “a collection of facts which no one remembers.” Practice planning and finishing a well-made personal project instead of abandoning it.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short walkthrough, browse the starting planets, search by name, open a profile, and use “Add planet” to create a new destination card and fill in its profile. Edit information and refresh to demonstrate persistence of edits and added entries. Click “Book the package” to reveal the travel confirmation, journey estimate, packing advice, and survival verdict—the memorable payoff.

## The POC Boundary
- Eight solar-system planets supplied initially, searchable by name and browsable as destination cards.
- An “Add planet” button to create a new destination card and fill in its profile; added entries persist in the same browser and join browsing and search.
- Destination profiles covering distance, travel time, distance from the Sun, atmosphere, and what being there would be like.
- Edit and add information within existing profiles, with changes saved in the same browser.
- Fictional booking reveal; travel-time estimates use an explicitly stated assumed spacecraft speed. Survival is a playful verdict rather than an unsupported scientific percentage.
- A small finished proof of concept sized for the hackathon's 2–4 hours of active work. Submission preparation later includes a short demo video and public GitHub repository; deployment is optional.

## Later
- A larger supplied catalogue beyond the solar system; manual planet additions are included now.
- Further production development after the proof of concept; no additional features are committed yet.

## Explicitly Cut
- Real checkout or purchasing: booking is the educational reveal.
- Accounts and cross-device storage: browser persistence is enough for this personal first version.
- Additional fun mechanics such as packing challenges, weight calculators, or passport stamps: the learner selected the holiday verdict as the first version's payoff.

Distance conventions, the assumed travel speed, editing behavior, and detailed presentation will be resolved in the PRD and technical plan.
