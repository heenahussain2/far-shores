Planetary
Travel Guide

Approved

A personal astronomy catalogue that starts with the eight solar-system planets as holiday destinations and lets you add your own planet entries.

The reason to build it

For you: go deeper into planets while keeping learning fun, memorable, and worth returning to.

Practice finishing a well-made personal project instead of letting it become another abandoned idea.

The memorable moment

Choose Book the package and reveal “Travel booked!”, a journey estimate, things to bring, and a playful survival verdict.

Real astronomy gives the fictional holiday its character.

Core loop → proof

From curiosity to an imaginary trip

You → browse or search → explore a profile → book a fictional trip → discover the verdict → explore again. Use “Add planet” to create a new card and fill in its profile. Added entries join browsing and search; additions and edits remain after refreshing.

A small project with a clear finish

Now · the complete first version
  • Browse the eight initial solar-system planet cards and search by name.
  • Use an “Add planet” button to create a new destination card and fill in its profile. Added entries join browsing and search and are saved in the same browser.
  • Open destination profiles with distance, travel time, distance from the Sun, atmosphere, and what being there would be like.
  • Edit and add information within existing profiles, saved in the same browser.
  • Reveal the travel confirmation, journey estimate, packing advice, and playful survival verdict through “Book the package.”
  • Label the trip as fictional; state the assumed spacecraft speed. Use a playful survival verdict rather than an unsupported scientific percentage.
Later · room to grow

A larger supplied catalogue beyond the solar system and further production development can follow a finished proof of concept. Manual planet additions are included now. No additional features are committed yet.

Cut · protect the finish line
  • Real checkout: booking is the educational reveal.
  • Accounts and cross-device storage: browser persistence fits personal use.
  • Extra games, weight calculators, and passport stamps: the selected holiday verdict is the first version’s payoff.

The one-minute proof

  1. Browse and search for a planet.
  2. Open its profile and explore its conditions.
  3. Add a planet card and fill in its profile.
  4. Edit information, refresh, and see additions and edits remain.
  5. Book the package and reveal the holiday verdict.

Identity & next decisions

“Welcome to your planetary travel guide,” “Explore destinations,” and “Enter your holiday destination” set the travel-guide tone.

Detailed styling, distance conventions, assumed travel speed, and editing behavior come in the PRD and technical plan.

Target: 2–4 hours of active hackathon work. Submission preparation includes a short demo video and public GitHub repository; deployment is optional.
